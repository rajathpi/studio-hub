Mascot Studio

Six tiny characters with cursor-following eyes — a parts bin for future tools.
Move your mouse around; they are watching.

Blob

The original. Round, pink, permanently pleased. On duty in Agreement Studio.

Cat

Ears, whiskers, attitude. Objectively the cutest, and it knows it.

Ghost

Floaty and a bit surprised. Moonlights as a Pac-Man extra — see below.

Robot

Glowing teal eyes and an antenna. For tools that mean business.

Owl

Huge eyes make it the best cursor tracker of the lot.

Doc buddy

A page with a folded corner and a face. Clippy's quieter cousin.

Everything runs locally — no libraries, no network, just SVG and a mousemove listener.
Borrow a character: copy its <svg> and the two little scripts below.
