      Cheatsheet
      

        

          
Character classes

          

            
.any char except newline (any with s)

            
\d  \Ddigit / non-digit

            
\w  \Wword char / non-word

            
\s  \Swhitespace / non-whitespace

            
[abc]any of a, b, c

            
[^abc]none of a, b, c

            
[a-z]range a to z

          

          
Anchors

          

            
^  $start / end (line with m)

            
\b  \Bword boundary / non-boundary

          

          
Flags

          

            
gall matches, not just first

            
icase-insensitive

            
m^ $ match per line

            
s. also matches newline

            
uunicode mode (\p{...})

          

        

        

          
Quantifiers

          

            
*  +  ?0+, 1+, 0-1 times

            
{3} {2,5} {2,}exact / range / at least

            
*?  +?lazy (as few as possible)

          

          
Groups

          

            
(abc)capture group

            
(?:abc)non-capturing group

            
(?<n>abc)named group «n»

            
\1  \k<n>backreference

            
a|balternation: a or b

          

          
Lookaround

          

            
(?=x) (?!x)followed / not followed by x

            
(?<=x) (?<!x)preceded / not preceded by x
