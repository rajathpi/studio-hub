  1. Open Background settings

  3. Set Choose a fit for your desktop image to Span.

Don’t use “Set as desktop background” from Photos or a right-click: Windows then uses Fill and squeezes the whole picture onto every screen.

macOS can’t stretch one picture across displays, so you got one picture per display. Open System Settings › Wallpaper and pick one for each.
