Monitors

Press Detect my screens (or add them by hand), then drag them into the order they stand on your desk. Lines carry on across the gaps between screens at true size. Check each diagonal: browsers can read resolution but not inches.

One last step

Saved to your Downloads.

  1. Open Background settings
  2. Next to Personalize your background, choose Picture, then Browse photos and pick the file.
  3. Set Choose a fit for your desktop image to Span.

Don’t use “Set as desktop background” from Photos or a right-click: Windows then squeezes the whole picture onto every screen.

macOS can’t stretch one picture across displays. Use Screens instead: it saves one picture per display, then System Settings › Wallpaper lets you pick one for each.
